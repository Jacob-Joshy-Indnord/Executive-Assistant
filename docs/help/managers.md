# For managers

[Back to the guide](README.md)

Your assistant has a page of its own. Only you can see it. Nothing your assistant writes
about a person's work reaches you until that person has confirmed it.

## Opening it for the first time

The first time you open the page, it asks **How you work**. Every part is optional:

- your working days and hours, and your time zone,
- the tone and sign-off of messages,
- your priorities and key people,
- how follow-ups work,
- when your status report comes.

Answer what you like and press **Save**, or press **Skip**. What you leave empty keeps the
usual setting: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00, and a report on Mondays at 08:00.

Your assistant does nothing until you save or skip. You can change your answers later in
**Settings**, under **How you work and the report**.

## Approval levels

Every kind of action starts at **With approval**: your assistant asks you first. Only you
can change that.

1. Open **Settings** and find **Approval levels**.
2. Choose a kind of action, such as **Add an action item**.
3. Choose a **Level**: **Not allowed**, **With approval** or **Without approval**.
4. For **Without approval**, you can set a daily number and the people it is for.
5. Press **Save level**. For **Without approval**, press **Confirm**.

Purchases, payments, bookings and agreements are never allowed.

What waits for you is under **Needs your approval** on your page. For each one, press
**Approve**, **Edit** (change the words, then approve) or **Decline**. **Approve all**
approves every request to an owner at once. What was done is under **Done for you**, with
**Undo** while it can still be undone.

## Action items

An action item is something an owner agreed to do for you.

1. Under **Action items**, press **Open**, then **Add an action item**.
2. Write **What is to be done**, name the **Owner**, and choose the **Due date**.
3. Press **Add item**.

The owner is asked to accept it. Your assistant then asks them for an update half-way to
the due date and on the due date, at 09:00. Each follow-up waits for your approval unless
you have changed its level.

When something needs you, it is under **Needs your attention**: for example, an item that
is late, blocked or not answered. Choose **Extend**, **Reassign**, **Take it up** or
**Close**. When an owner asks for another date, choose **Use the new date** or **Keep the
first date**.

## Status reports

1. Open **Settings** and find **Projects followed**.
2. Press **Follow** beside a project. Name **Who confirms its status**, or leave it empty
   for the project's owner. Press **Follow** again.

Before each report, your assistant drafts a statement for each project that has news. It
then asks the person who confirms it. They confirm it, correct it, or write their own.

Your report shows one line for each project: its colour in words, the reason and its mark.
See [Statement marks](statement-marks.md). Under **Project status**, press **Open** to see
the lines and the reports made, or press **Report now**.

## Pausing

In **Settings**, under **Pause**, press **Pause**. Nothing is started, sent or carried out
until you press **Resume**. When you resume, what fell due while you were away is done
once.

Your organisation can also pause all virtual employees. Your page then says so, and only
your organisation can resume.
