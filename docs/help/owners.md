# For owners

[Back to the guide](README.md)

A manager's assistant may ask you to do something, or to say how your work is going. You
answer on the **Requests for you** page. You speak for your own work: nothing about it
reaches the manager until you have confirmed it.

## Accepting an action item

Under **Asked of you**, a manager asks you to accept an action item. Choose one:

- **Accept**: you will do it by the due date.
- **Propose another date**: choose the **Date you can do it by**, give a **Reason**, and
  press **Propose date**.
- **Decline**: give a **Reason** and press **Decline**.

## Updating

When you are asked for an update, press **Update**. You can also update any item under
**Your action items** without being asked.

1. Choose **On track**, **Done** or **Blocked**.
2. Add a **Note** if you like, and **Another date** if you need one.
3. Press **Send update**.

The manager sees your words exactly as you wrote them, with the time.

## Confirming a status

A manager may ask you for the status of a project.

- **When the assistant drafted it**, read it. Choose the **Colour** and change any words
  that are not right. Press **Confirm** if the words are right, or **Confirm with changes**.
- **When there is no draft**, choose the **Colour** and write the **Reason**. **What
  changed** and **What comes next** are optional. Press **Give status**.

You always choose the colour yourself. Each colour shows what it means in your
organisation.

## Correcting

Your statements are under **Your confirmed statements**. To correct one, press **Correct**,
change it, and press **Save correction**. The correction is added as a new version, and both versions are
kept.

What each mark means is in [Statement marks](statement-marks.md).
